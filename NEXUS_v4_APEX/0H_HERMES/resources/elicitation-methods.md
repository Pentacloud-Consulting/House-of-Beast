## Elicitation Methods (Library)

Used by HERMES before surfacing a proposal to reduce low-signal changes.

1) Reverse brainstorm: how could this proposal be made worse?
2) Five whys: why does the failure recur?
3) Inversion: what's the opposite recommendation, and when is it valid?
4) Bounding: smallest/largest scope where it applies
5) Pre-mortem: assume it fails after applying; what likely went wrong?
